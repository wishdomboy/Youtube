Why Do Cyber Frauds Work?
From a phone call to an AI deepfake, every scam plays the same game.
WHAT
What they want
•Your money, directly
•OTP, PIN and passwords
•Your identity documents
•Access to phone or account
•Company secrets and IP
•Your silence: "tell no one"
WHY
Why they do it
•Quick money, low risk
•Hard to trace, hard to catch
•One script, millions of victims
•Stolen data is resold
•Revenge, espionage, disruption
HOW

•Greed: "you won a prize"
•Urgency: "do it right now"
•Authority: fake police, boss
•Love and loneliness

Beware the Basic Scams
18 everyday cyber frauds, and how to stay safe
Pause. Think. Verify.
Phone call scams
1
KYC / bank block call
"Account closing today, share OTP."
2
Card block call
Asks for card number, CVV or PIN.
3
Fake loan / insurance
"Pre-approved loan, pay a fee first."
4
Fake customer care
Number from Google, then asks you to install an app.
5
Call-merge scam
Asks you to merge calls to catch your OTP.
Red flag: urgency + OTP request
UPI & payment tricks
6
Collect request
You think you receive money, you actually pay.
7
"Galti se paise bhej diye"
They send money, then ask you to return it.
8
QR code scam
Scanning a QR only ever sends money.
9
OLX "army officer"
A buyer sends you a QR to "pay" you.
Remember: PIN or scan = you are paying, never receiving
Lure & reward scams
10
Fake lottery / KBC
You "won", but must pay a tax or fee.
11
Job & "like and earn"
Small payouts first, then a big deposit.
12
Gift / parcel scam
"Customs held your gift, pay clearance."
Real prizes never ask you to pay first
Trust & message scams
13
Romance / matrimonial
Fake profile, then an "emergency, send money".
14
Sextortion
Unknown video call, recorded clip, blackmail.
15
Charity fraud
Fake donation links and accounts.
16
Relative in trouble
"Beta, accident ho gaya, send money now."
17
Electricity-bill SMS
"Pay today or power cut."
18
Fake courier SMS
Link to update address or pay a small fee.
Call the person on a number you already know
3 Golden Rules
1
Never share OTP or PIN
Not with the bank, not with "police", not with anyone.
2
Pause. Think. Verify.
Scammers rush you. Call the official number yourself.
3
Report fast: dial 1930
Or visit cybercrime.gov.in. Quick reports save money.
Urgency + fear + greed = scam. Stay calm, stay safe!
